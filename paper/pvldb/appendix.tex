\section{BIRD refusal reasons}
Each gold query alone, fresh session, $k=3$. Financial, then debit card. Rank or group by a protected value: \Nfincatrank, \Ndebitcatrank. Filter on too few entities: \Nfincatthreshold, \Ndebitcatthreshold. Aggregate under a protected filter: \Nfincatweighted, \Ndebitcatweighted. Filtered count over a join: \Nfincatfanout, \Ndebitcatfanout. Aggregate of an expression: \Nfincatexpression, \Ndebitcatexpression. Aggregate over too few entities: \Nfincataggregate, \Ndebitcataggregate. Shape cannot be audited: \Nfincatopaque, \Ndebitcatopaque.
\section{Query portfolio and rank test}
No language model, 1{,}500 customers, \Nrevn{} targets, 100 queries. Minimum group size and no audit recovered all \Nrevn{}. Nested pairs recovered all \Nrevn{} and stopped bisection. The linear audit recovered none (Wilson upper bound \Nrevwilson\%). A two-customer sum matched both balances in \Nrevpairsum{} sessions and determined \Nrevpairexact{} individuals. \Nrevfloattrials{} integer-weight trials had \Nrevfloatmiss{} float-versus-rational disagreements. Weights near $10^{9}$ are a counterexample to the $10^{-9}$ test.
\section{DuckDB and Laplace}
Aggregated key weights on one million customers: \Nrevduckms{}~ms median. \Nrevduckhist{} range updates, maximum \Nrevduckmax{}~ms. Laplace plus a noisy count of at least 3, basic composition, not OpenDP: at $\varepsilon=100$, median relative error \Nrevdperr\% and difference-within-10\% on \Nrevdpatt\% of draws.
